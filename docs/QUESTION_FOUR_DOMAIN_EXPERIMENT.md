# Four-domain question-generation feasibility experiment

The evaluated pipeline produced questions across Spanish, sourdough baking and photography, but it still accepted a concrete wrong Spanish rule and introduced faulty teaching feedback. The fictional-rules case timed out before returning questions. This experiment does **not** qualify the pipeline as correct, adequately challenging, or reliable for arbitrary goals.

The run used the unchanged four remaining fixture goals after the generalized layout prompt change. Its immutable source revision was `5957f4658ab7a0534f1de9c3cf43429ccdd9662c`. The prospective plan, exact requests, prompts, settings, authorization, provider requests/responses/errors and byte-preserving blind reviews are archived in [the raw evidence](evidence/four-domain-feasibility-20260906.json). [The independent adjudication](evidence/four-domain-adjudication-20260906.json) joins every original candidate to its author key, solver record, reviewer verdict, four choice explanations and returned question. It preserves every frozen blind rating.

“Best-listed” is a contextual judgment, not proof that a practical diagnosis is uniquely determined by all possible omitted conditions. In particular, the sourdough bulk-end question lacks a formula-specific endpoint, and the progressive-weakening question lacks enough controls to establish acid-driven gluten breakdown conclusively. The frozen review treats their offered answers as the best practical choices; the later explanation audit preserves those limits rather than converting them into universal rules. These assistant assessments are a small feasibility sample, not expert-panel or learner-calibrated accuracy estimates.

There were 21 raw occurrences and 21 unique exact-content items. Every candidate was parsed and passed runtime sanitization. The reviewer marked **all 21 valid**. Eight were removed only by the difficulty floor; none was rejected as factually invalid. Thirteen reached the returned bank. Eleven returned keys matched a supported best-listed answer in the blind review; the other two were the Spanish false-premise item and an underqualified Spanish answer. A correct key did not ensure correct feedback.

A clear retained failure is `9e2995f0f650daddc28917f0cfa7d9a3a3836525c9c7453fda4a11c53188af37`. It presents “No dudo que Juan sepa la respuesta” as a mood error and requires changing *sepa* to *sabe*. The author, option-blind solver and final reviewer all asserted a mandatory-indicative rule. Standard Spanish permits both moods after negated doubt; the Instituto Cervantes curriculum explicitly includes *no dudo* followed by a subjunctive example, and a corpus study documents both forms. The returned main explanation and correct-choice feedback repeat the false rule. [Cervantes curriculum](https://cvc.cervantes.es/ensenanza/biblioteca_ele/plan_curricular/niveles/05_funciones_inventario_b1-b2.htm), [Cervantes-hosted corpus study](https://cvc.cervantes.es/ensenanza/biblioteca_ele/aepe/pdf/congreso_40/congreso_40_37.pdf).

Two other Spanish items require a different judgment. `8554c97b447cd893d53b3924fa72c20f60d0147d63a86cf6cf7ef15e4ee742db`, which was retained, turns nonspecific reference in *Busco un profesor que hable japonés* into an unsupported claim that María has not found such a professor yet. She could know several while seeking any suitable available one. `f8d1db9c8dfa108f0ee0f8a64fe5eda4a68dab1ac59b223ff6f955042b48a992` treats subjunctive after *aunque* as necessarily uncertain, overlooking its use for an already-known fact conceded as irrelevant. The latter was labeled valid and removed for difficulty 2. [RAE mood guidance](https://www.rae.es/libro-estilo-lengua-espa%C3%B1ola/el-modo-indicativo-o-subjuntivo), [Cervantes grammar inventory](https://cvc.cervantes.es/ensenanza/biblioteca_ele/plan_curricular/niveles/02_gramatica_inventario_b1-b2.htm).

The feedback audit found specific errors beyond keys. Among 13 returned main explanations, two contain clear incorrect claims: the Spanish rule above and the photography group-photo explanation. Among 52 returned choice explanations, three contain clear incorrect claims: the same Spanish rule, a sourdough aroma claim, and a shutter-speed claim. These are claim-level findings, with overlapping items. Additional unsupported inferences and missing qualifications are recorded separately; they are not all counted as wrong answers.

- In retained `591ba4316f9396aceba7ee39b206808ff9352572192ececc8961ec438865e9eb`, the main explanation says no other listed change affects depth of field, although switching to an 85 mm lens is a listed change. Its own distractor explanation correctly says the longer focal length changes depth of field. Another choice explanation says shutter speed only changes exposure, omitting motion rendering. Stopping down is still the best listed answer. [Canon depth-of-field explanation](https://files.canon-europe.com/files/webcontent/rf-lens-world/knowledge/depth-of-field/index.html), [Canon exposure controls](https://www.canon.ca/CanonOutsideOfAuto/tips?lang=en).
- In retained `a062c9d09f72efa6ba93abd37257e87051c5298bc8755308b13f7567d41c8cfa`, the whole-wheat starter answer is supported, but the reviewer adds that a collapsing starter would not develop a stronger sour aroma. Hungry, past-peak starters can develop sharper vinegar aromas. [King Arthur starter troubleshooting](https://shop.kingarthurbaking.com/content/recipes/SourdoughBooklet_2_2026.pdf).
- Retained sourdough `5b2b53f8d39b68944949ac0cc55d5d2342f7b6ca6ae87aacd33e0befa7ac6e58` gains an unsupported claim that increased preferment accelerates fermentation proportionally and that the given rise equals the normal recipe endpoint. Neither exact proportionality nor that endpoint was supplied. Bulk timing depends on dough and process conditions. [King Arthur bulk fermentation guidance](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/blog/2019/07/22/bulk-fermentation).
- Retained photography `5fe81d1e0f877576c2d2cc7e181f56248171424e60ec02df95b2207ef7f72be8` turns a common overexposure risk into categorical claims about matrix metering and insufficient dynamic range. Matrix metering uses more than a simple scene average, and the stem gives no numerical contrast or sensor range. The blind answer remains a plausible best-listed outcome; the categorical feedback is not justified. [Nikon metering guidance](https://www.nikonusa.com/learn-and-explore/c/products-and-innovation/using-the-highlight-weighted-metering-mode), [Nikon matrix-metering description](https://www.nikonusa.com/fileuploads/pdfs/D3_brochure.pdf).

The full audit also preserves faulty feedback on discarded questions, including a claim that a stationary background must blur without panning and a claim that underexposure cannot coexist with a clipped right histogram peak. Those did not reach the returned bank. Their rejection was by difficulty, not because the reviewer recognized the incorrect feedback.

Difficulty remained weak. Under the shared frozen 1–5 rubric, 11 of the 21 raw items were estimated at level 2 and 10 at level 3; none required level 4 or 5 reasoning. Five of the 13 returned items were estimated at level 2 despite the requested minimum of 3. The author labeled every item 3; the reviewer assigned 3 or 4 to every retained item. These independent estimates test whether scenario details change the reasoning, rather than crediting length, specialist vocabulary or a numeric label. This run does not test adaptive learning over repeated learner responses.

The author, solver and reviewer all requested `us.anthropic.claude-opus-4-6-v1`, adaptive thinking, high effort and 16,000 output tokens. Every one of the 18 successful responses ended with `end_turn` and reported one reasoning-content block. No successful call ended for an output-token limit; the largest reported output was 5,995 tokens. Successful responses reported 31,275 input and 43,505 output tokens in total. The timed-out call supplied no usage, so those figures are not a complete billing estimate. The evaluated errors occurred despite reasoning being requested and observed; this run supplies no evidence that increasing the output ceiling would fix them.

All raw stems were at most 260 characters, and choices at most 105, below the author’s 320/140 limits. No literal code or layout defect occurred in these prose-only candidates. The observed Spanish mistakes and misleading feedback were not produced by clipping a returned stem. This does not establish that the length limits are adequate for more demanding scenarios or source-based tasks: Morrow returned no content and cannot answer that question.

The prospective cap was 24 calls, six per independent goal, one serial job each. Provider failure ended the affected goal without retry or resume; unused calls were not reassigned. The run consumed 19 calls and reached terminal exit 1 after all four goals were attempted. Morrow’s first author call failed with `ReadTimeoutError` after approximately 100 seconds. The three completed goals took 202.355, 241.633 and 267.550 seconds; Morrow took 100.007 seconds. No further model calls were made. The experiment says nothing about Morrow question correctness, and the timeout’s underlying cause remains unknown.

An initial startup attempt used a Python interpreter without `boto3` and failed during client construction with **zero provider dispatches**. The approved dependency-only amendment changed the interpreter to Python 3.12.11 with boto3/botocore 1.43.89 and used a fresh output directory. Source, prompts, requests, model settings, timeout, rubric and budget remained fixed. Imports and client construction were preflighted without a model call. This was not a provider retry. The original empty artifacts, authorization and amendment are preserved in the archive. Offline archive checks reproduced every embedded file hash and all frozen review bytes, verified the immutable revision and settings, joined all 21 author/reviewer records and 84 choice explanations, reproduced the report counts and call limits, and checked the committed artifacts for credential patterns. The evidence supports a narrower next requirement: verify the complete keyed claim and the teaching feedback, preserve exceptions and uncertainty, and measure actual cognitive difficulty independently. Model agreement alone did not supply correctness here. No runtime promotion, deployment, queue execution, learner simulation or app-blocking test is part of this milestone.

# Constructing questions from explicit Boolean rules

This is an additional experimental question family, not a replacement for general question generation. It constructs an answer from a bounded rule system instead of asking successive model calls to agree about an already-written answer. It is not connected to production generation, grading, inventory or adaptive mastery updates.

The [source-first experiment](QUESTION_SOURCE_AUTHORING_EXPERIMENT.md) showed missing conditions and unsupported feedback even with relevant references. The [grounding probe](QUESTION_WEB_GROUNDING_PROBE.md) subsequently demonstrated live search access, but exposed no source passages and still produced an inaccurate explanation. Those results motivate moving answer construction and the associated explanation into code where the question has an exact, bounded interpretation.

## Conditional guarantee and boundaries

The constructor accepts four to eight named Boolean atoms, signed case facts and one to eight one-way rules, each with one or two signed antecedents. It enumerates all assignments in that vocabulary (at most 256) and keeps only those satisfying the displayed premises. An absent fact is unknown; it is not silently false. Contradictory premises cannot produce a question by vacuous entailment.

Every offered conclusion is evaluated against every remaining assignment. A key must follow in all of them, and each distractor must have a counterexample that obeys every rule and stated fact. The question asks what **must follow**: a distractor may be true in some cases while still failing to answer that question. Feedback must describe that actual distinction, rather than falsely claim the distractor is impossible.

The same structured premises supply the rendered stem, answer choices and proof/counterexample feedback. No second free-writing model call can add a causal mechanism or erase a premise afterward. Complete rendered fields must fit the existing limits of 320 stem characters, 140 per choice, 420 for the main explanation and 280 per choice explanation; otherwise the constructor returns an unsupported result. It never truncates a condition to fit.

These guarantees are conditional on the declared formal interpretation. They do not prove that English labels have no unstated semantic relationships, that a source was translated into the rules correctly, or that a rule is an accurate real-world fact. A model-provided claim of qualification would not establish any of those properties. Source-to-rule qualification and ordinary subject validity remain separate work before a lesson could enter a production registry.

The structural gate requires a non-given answer with minimum forward derivation depth at least two. It also enumerates subsets of the stated rules (at most 256) and requires that no subset of zero or one rule can entail the key, including through contraposition. These are structural checks, not calibrated difficulty or a measurement of a learner's shortest reasoning process. Distinct truth patterns for distractors are not evidence that learners find them plausible. Neither a source title nor a change of vocabulary establishes subject mastery or transfer. The module assigns neither a runtime verification version nor a learner difficulty rating.

Distractors originate from reversing an implication, dropping a required conjunct, negating a derived conclusion, or declaring an unknown prerequisite absent. The constructor independently checks each resulting literal and excludes equivalent truth patterns. It cannot fill a missing option with an unrelated unused variable. If three distinct alternatives cannot be supported by these construction rules, the question is unsupported.

The output includes the full normalized rule specification, exact content hashes, the key's derivation, its minimum entailing rule subset, and a truth vector and counterexample for each distractor. Certificate checking reconstructs the question and compares every field, including feedback. A hash mismatch invalidates that exact association; a matching hash is not a signature or permission to present an unqualified subject lesson.

## Research basis

[LINC](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.313/) separates natural-language formalization from logical inference and reports both benefits and remaining formalization errors. [Logic-LM](https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-emnlp.248/) similarly combines language models with symbolic solvers. These results motivate an independently checkable inference boundary; they do not establish Checkpoint's coverage or quality. Rendering the displayed exercise from the same structured rules removes one particular translation boundary, while factual accuracy and the intended meaning of those rules remain unproven.

## Implementation and verification

[The constructor](../backend/bedrock-question-service/question_rule_construction.py) is a pure module with two public entry points: `construct_rule_question(spec)` returns a question and its scoped certificate, or raises `RuleConstructionError`; `verify_rule_certificate(question, certificate)` reconstructs and compares the complete result. Unknown object fields, non-Boolean values, oversized specifications, duplicate or canonically colliding labels, and labels containing control or line-separator characters are rejected. Opaque quoted labels retain their original Unicode. If a candidate's full explanation exceeds its limit, another eligible candidate may be tried; text is never clipped.

The first example stipulates `A -> B`, `B -> C`, `D -> B` and `E -> C`, with `A` given. Code constructs `C is true.` as the key, explains the two forward rule applications, and offers `C is false.`, `D is true.` and `E is true.` as alternatives. The permitted assignment `A=true, B=true, C=true, D=false, E=false` disproves each alternative as a conclusion that must follow. This explanation makes no claim about whether a parcel is actually safe to ship or whether any real-world tag implies readiness.

An independent reviewer prepared fourteen structured cases before invoking the implementation: three compact candidate lessons using parcel, rehearsal and nursery-tag vocabulary, and eleven additional controls. Root separately parsed the actual rendered stems, choices and counterexample feedback, enumerated their possible assignments, and replayed the certificate's forward trace against the displayed rules. All input premises had to survive rendering unchanged, every key had to be uniquely entailed, and each visible counterexample had to satisfy the complete displayed rule system. The separate focused tests additionally parse and replay the learner-visible main explanation; that is distinct from the root archive's certificate-trace check.

Six of the fourteen cases constructed items and passed these checks; eight returned unsupported. The three vocabulary examples all constructed, with stems of 236, 272 and 275 characters. Supported controls retained a negative key, preserved one-way inference, and excluded equivalent distractors. Unsupported cases included missing positive or negative conditions, contradictory premises, an unseeded cycle, direct shortcuts and duplicate labels. Unsupported does not mean factually wrong: some valid rule systems cannot supply this family's required derivation and three distinct distractors.

The independent run also checked 128 deterministic signed/conjunctive variants. All constructed outputs had one entailed key, three distinct truth patterns, mechanically motivated alternatives, correct visible counterexamples and valid forward traces. It independently found the smallest entailing rule subset and rejected six content/evidence mutations per output. These are software stress checks of two synthetic graph patterns, not 128 fresh goals, evidence of subject coverage, or a production accuracy estimate.

## Remaining work for the actual learning experience

The examples deliberately call their labels opaque and ask about stipulated Boolean premises. They demonstrate a sound inference component, not instruction in shipping, music or horticulture. The author model was not called to propose lessons in this milestone; no source-to-rule translator, qualified lesson registry or adaptive integration was established. There was no learner or educator study of clarity, distractor plausibility or difficulty.

A production use must establish that the lesson rules accurately express the relevant source and learning objective, and that practicing their application teaches the intended skill. It must also preserve the generated content through presentation and grading, diversify exercises, measure challenge and progression, and qualify usable inventory within operational limits. Arbitrary factual, quantitative and comparative MCQs still need their corresponding evidence and verification paths. The prior general solver failures remain unresolved; this bounded family does not redefine the broader correctness goal or qualify a release.

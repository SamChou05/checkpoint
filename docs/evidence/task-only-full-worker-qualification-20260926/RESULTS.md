**Decision: failed; do not enable this worker route or deploy it as the default.** The frozen [plan](plan.json) (SHA-256 `a8e9e39470704e3a83ec96febe6b402acb0df40fbbdb114de267265725bdf393`) ran once against Bedrock. Its [capture](capture.json) (SHA-256 `5278562961290acc3fac998a5429797aba55c100a3e9cc6a59a58b62692007f4`) records all three original five-slot jobs, eight reserved/attempted calls, and no fallback, retry, replacement, or resume. The three jobs stayed within their individual 240-second and six-call limits. Ten of fifteen original questions returned, below the prespecified **14/15** threshold.

The mixed failure is more specific than an ordinary deadline problem. Its visible native response contains **37 complete top-level question rows** (three quantitative and 34 prose) and begins a 38th despite `targetCount: 5`. The unbounded array grammar required a JSON shape but did not constrain item count. The response reached 32,871 visible characters, ended with an unterminated string, and was rejected in full; no partial row was admitted or keyed. This one response demonstrates a runaway author on this request, not a population failure rate or a causal explanation for why the model generated so many rows. The [mixed diagnostic](DIAGNOSTIC_MIXED.md) examines request and contract details separately.

The 15-slot [keyless worksheet](blind-worksheet.json) (SHA-256 `e54530bfd579fda94abe65e7aff8cc49382eb51db9fc4c02affb084ca3143a08`) kept five unavailable placeholders. Two independent reviewers evaluated all ten returned stems, selected their best answers, and judged every one of each item's six answer-choice pairs before the captured keys or teaching were joined. Their [reviews and hashes were durably locked](blind-lock.json) first. The [post-lock join](unblinded-review.json) shows **10/10 captured keys selected by each reviewer**, **60/60 pairs judged distinct by each**, and no missing premise or unresolved answer uncertainty. These are ten observed sound items, not evidence of dependable fifteen-item throughput.

Independent [numerical](POST_REVIEW_NUMERICAL.md) and [Python](POST_REVIEW_PYTHON.md) audits found the returned teaching sound. All five numerical learner fields replay exactly from each trusted original typed task through the deterministic constructor/compiler, with rationally distinct options, correct keys, and policy-8 stamps. The separate Python audit checked all five answers and operand-return `and`/`or` explanations against Python 3.12, with immutable authored mains and policy-7 provenance. The numerical reviewer vetoed one mathematically valid, explicitly in-scope `4.5 × 0.8` task; this removed a decimal example and consumed a top-up but did not release a wrong answer. Two returned numerical topic labels are clipped and some fraction distractor feedback is generic, without changing answer validity in this sample.

The route also misses its other prospective gates: only **five compiled** returns versus six required, **zero mixed compiled** returns versus two required, and no mixed English prose return. The Python job does include operand-return Boolean items. The source option remains exact-goal gated and off by default. The verified deployment plumbing is also opt-in and was not used to deploy this trial; the live TestFlight stack inspected on September 26 remained on the September 11 legacy packages. No SQS, DynamoDB, inventory-refill, installed-app, or post-deployment behavior is qualified by this local harness run.

# Seven authored probability questions: keys sound, difficulty misses

The [keyless worksheet](worksheet.json) was frozen at SHA-256
`19b4cd661ba5cb59c99d65fc7c16e1d2c34ddfb353fa54ba1106f03060f2f728`.
It contains the seven sanitized author rows from the immutable [trial-02
capture](../generic-reserve-seven-probe-v2-20260927/RESULTS.md), with choices
shuffled and source keys kept outside Git. The full worker trial returned
**0/5** because Bedrock rejected the seven-row solver request, so these are
**author-only** findings.

Two reviewers saw only the worksheet and rubric before saving and hashing
their independent assessments. [Review A](review-a.json) has SHA-256
`4f2ad65b7eb913e2446f0cf2933d7d36e1db4983c4e76f2dfdf0bdf33b61473f`;
[review B](review-b.json) has SHA-256
`b9330adc6ea211190d6778f17767095995ab6df06783ad17e0852639fbbd24c4`.
Both selected the private source key for **7/7** rows and found all **42/42**
within-item unordered choice pairs meaningfully different. Neither found a
second defensible answer. A marked one of 21 cross-item pairs strongly
repetitive (G01/G02); B marked none. This is an honest reviewer disagreement
about bank similarity.

The request asked for introductory probability difficulty 2. Both reviewers
rated **G01, G03, and G06 at difficulty 1**. A rated the other four at 2; B
rated G05 at 3 and the remaining three at 2. Thus a strict difficulty-2 gate
cannot admit five of these seven rows even though their keys and choices are
sound. Both noted weak or generic distractors on some items. A also judged
G04 not fully self-contained because sections labeled with dollar amounts
were not explicitly defined as payouts; B accepted the implied meaning. A
post-lock arithmetic read found the seven author explanations consistent
with their selected keys, while the G04 wording issue remains.

This sample separates two problems: native authoring can produce one clear
answer and four distinct choices, but it does not reliably hit the requested
difficulty or remove every semantic concern. The absent solver and reviewer
calls mean these candidates were never verified or returned. Neither this
review nor a transport repair qualifies the generic route for production.

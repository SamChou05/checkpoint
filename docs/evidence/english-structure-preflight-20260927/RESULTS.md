# Alternate English agreement structures: blind offline review

Eight randomized, answer-hidden items sampled the old and added English
scenes in the exact mapped slots. Choices were shuffled separately. Two
independent reviewers saw only the [worksheet](worksheet.json) (SHA-256
`f4e8d70c19d42ab406677c0fbdc67d2268747ce81686cb10416d065b0d9a518a`),
then saved [review A](review-a.json) and [review B](review-b.json) before the
[private key/scene map](private.json) was read. Both chose all **8/8** code-owned
keys, found one answer and four distinct options per item, and rated each
standalone question difficulty 2. Their answers matched after the lock.

The new inverted-subject and `a number`/`the number` scenes are distinguishable
from the previous proximity and compound/distributive mechanisms in this
small worksheet. Both reviewers nevertheless identified four strong pairs
within the eight, because each mechanism has multiple scenes and every item
uses the same two-blank ordered-pair format. This preflight supports exact
keys and a first-refill structural change; it does not establish long-bank
novelty, broader English coverage, full worker yield, or production readiness.
The mapped route remains disabled.

# Mixed math response-format blind review: valid keys, limited novelty

The [frozen keyless worksheet](worksheet.json) interleaved six examples from
the quarantined level-2 bank with three exact-gap comparison items and three
worked-reasoning items from standalone, non-production constructors. Its
SHA-256 was
`0da3ddc7f43b28e2eb1ee296211999fa7c97045ff3ded9af33ad882e38080517`.
The private source/key map remained outside Git until both reviews were saved
and hashed. [Review A](review-a.json) is SHA-256
`6c6c97df0f50cef2cc89d354ce39335b7220509f2d4091e7828b94ce93ee26c3`;
[review B](review-b.json) is SHA-256
`77542524e9e8ae69c0e27151e67b87a63fb3870204b60d3e75ed9c145826b6e7`.

Both independent reviewers inferred the private key for **12/12** items,
found **72/72** within-item choice pairs meaningfully different, and rated
each item difficulty 2 or 3. Neither found an unsupported selected worked step
or an answer cue in an individual item. These are useful one-key and choice
results for the samples, not a guarantee across all constructor variants.

They disagreed about the breadth of bank repetition but agreed on its core.
Review A marked **7/66** cross-item pairs strong; review B marked **12/66**.
All seven A pairs were in B's set. Both found that each of the three
comparison items repeats the same direction-plus-gap decision and answer
format, and that each of the three worked-evaluation items repeats the same
precedence-and-error-pattern response format. Both also marked the old
linear-equation pair M07/M11 strong. B additionally grouped the old
numeric-selection and count-in-domain items by their repeated response
format. Changing operands or preliminary arithmetic does not make the
learner's central decision new.

The old bare-number alternatives are mostly nearby guesses or adjacent
counts rather than diagnostic misconception choices. Review B also noted
programming-style `!=`/`<=` and repeated “unitless number” boilerplate in
some stems. These quality concerns persist despite semantic distinction
within each four-choice set.

This is an **offline content test**. Neither new constructor is wired into the
author schema or worker, and this review says nothing about live Bedrock
schema acceptance, reviewer yield, deadlines, or 40/80-question bank
capacity. More independently different solving decisions and response formats
are needed before promotion.

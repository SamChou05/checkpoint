# Current-source full-worker qualification: sound returns, failed yield

The one-shot successor trial ran the current source's unmodified generation worker on three fixed five-question assignments. It returned **9/15** original slots: numerical **0/5**, Python **5/5**, and mixed **4/5**. Both independent keyless reviewers found a unique answer and distinct choice meanings for every returned item, and the post-lock teaching audit found all nine usable. The frozen release gate nevertheless fails at **9/15 < 14/15**, with no numerical returns and only **three** compiled items against the required six. No default, deployment, or inventory policy changed.

| Frozen gate | Requirement | Observed | Result |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Original slots returned | at least 14/15 | 9/15 | **Fail** |
| Per-job returns | at least 4/5 each | 0/5 numerical; 5/5 Python; 4/5 mixed | **Fail** |
| Compiled returns | at least 6 total, including 4 numerical and 2 mixed | 3 total; 0 numerical, 3 mixed | **Fail** |
| Mixed English prose | at least 1 returned | 1 | Pass |
| Python `and`/`or` operand-return exposure | at least 1 returned | `0 or 'hello'`, with the final-operand fallback taught correctly | Pass |
| Returned learner content | every item sound, unique, distinct, in scope, at least difficulty 2 | 9/9 under two blind answer reviews and separate teaching audits | Pass for returned items only |
| Time and calls | 240 seconds and six reservations per job; 18 total | 188.928, 138.062, 234.238 seconds; 1, 3, 6 calls; 10 total | Pass |

The numerical author returned a native response after **188.839 seconds** with `stopReason=max_tokens` and exactly **16,000 output tokens**. Its visible JSON text ended inside a string and could not be decoded into a question batch. The runtime correctly recorded `output_truncated` and failed the numerical job closed; it did not accept a partial item or invent a key. The capture does not establish why the model spent the allowance, or whether changing its reasoning setting, request size, or token ceiling would improve quality. In the mixed job, four items survived its first author → solver → reviewer pass; a later one-slot reviewer read timed out after dispatch. The four already verified returns were preserved.

The [independent structural replay](STRUCTURAL_REPLAY.md) rechecked the frozen source and evidence hashes, all ten exact requests and native contracts, 15 original slots, deadlines, admission records, and three compiled provenance chains without a provider call. It reproduced 0/5, 5/5, and 4/5. Nine responses report **37,851 input and 40,136 output tokens**; the timed-out tenth has unknown usage, not zero. Eight of the 18 reserved call slots were unattempted.

The [keyless worksheet](blind-worksheet.json) preserved all 15 original slots, with six explicit unavailable placeholders. Two independent assessors [locked their judgments](blind-lock.json) on every available choice and all six unordered choice pairs before the [keys were joined](unblinded-review.json). Their literal answers matched the explicit keys for **all nine** returned items; neither reviewer credited an unavailable slot. The [Python teaching audit](POST_REVIEW_PYTHON.md) found 5/5 sound policy-7 authored mains, including the complete Boolean `or` rule, with empty per-choice feedback. The [mixed teaching audit](POST_REVIEW_MIXED.md) found 4/4 sound returns: three policy-8 arithmetic items recompiled all five learner fields exactly from trusted original tasks, and one policy-7 English item retained its authored main byte-for-byte. The two fraction items share a stem shell, and the English item's returned difficulty 3 is generous, but their distinct operations and minimum difficulty 2 passed the frozen checks.

This is a one-run qualification, not a population accuracy estimate. It uses Sonnet 4.6 with native structured output, adaptive/high reasoning, 16,000 combined tokens, array author cardinality, the constructed-quantitative route, authored-solution feedback, and a 200-second trial read allowance clamped to each job's deadline. It did not run the deployed Lambda, SQS, DynamoDB, or inventory refill. The earlier longer-read trial returned 13/15, but its source and generated items differed, so that comparison is descriptive rather than causal. The active worker and client defaults remain unchanged; the inspected TestFlight package is still the legacy September 11 deployment.

The first [frozen plan](../current-source-worker-qualification-20260926/plan.json) produced a [zero-call setup abort](../current-source-worker-qualification-20260926/SETUP_ABORT.md) because its AWS CLI credential snapshot had too little lifetime. This successor was separately reviewed, passed **53 socket-blocked harness tests and 32 service tests**, and used one [pretrial freshness check](launch-precheck.json) before its [frozen plan](plan.json). That check verified the intended account and 898 seconds of credential life, with one recorded STS request, one observed Botocore Signin refresh, an unobserved CLI Signin count bounded at zero or one, and zero model calls. Its pretrial AWS operations are separate from the ten trial Converse requests. [Independent preflight](INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md) approved the one-shot launch.

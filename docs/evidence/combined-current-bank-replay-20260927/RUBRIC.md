# Blind review instructions for the combined 20-item sample

Read only `worksheet.json` until your independent judgments are locked to its
SHA-256. For each item, choose the one correct letter, or mark ambiguous/no
correct answer. Assess whether all four choices are meaningfully different,
whether the prompt and choices are natural and self-contained, and whether
the item is near the requested difficulty 2–3. Review every one of the 190
cross-item pairs for repeated reasoning or near-duplicate question form;
distinguish strong, moderate, and low overlap. Explain each strong overlap.

The worksheet deliberately omits all answer keys, source tasks, family labels,
and bank-order metadata. Its private map must remain sealed until the review
is written and locked. This is an offline constructor sample, not a live
model or full-worker acceptance test.

# Keyless comparison-task worksheet

Choose one answer for each question. For each item, assess whether exactly one choice is correct, whether the other three represent distinct plausible errors, and whether the question fits level 2. Also assess whether the five questions feel repetitive as a bank.

## C01

Let A = 2/3 + 4/6 and B = 2/4 + 4/5. Which is larger, and by exactly how much?

A. A is larger than B by 1/6.
B. B is larger than A by 1/6.
C. A is larger than B by 1/30.
D. B is larger than A by 1/30.

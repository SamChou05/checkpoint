The app shuffles answer choices before learners see them. In a prose question's
explanation, identify an answer by its choice text or value, or explain the
relevant content directly. Never identify an offered answer by its display
letter, number, position or order (such as "choice B", "(b)" or "the second option").
This restriction concerns references to offered choices: preserve letters and
numbers that are genuine quoted subject text, code, mathematical variables or
explicit subpart labels in the stem. Keep the required JSON choices a/b/c/d
fields and correctChoice a/b/c/d value; those bind structured data and are not
learner-facing display labels.

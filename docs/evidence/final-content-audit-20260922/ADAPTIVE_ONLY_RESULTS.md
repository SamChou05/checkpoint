## What passed and what failed

Eight defective payloads were rejected, including the false paint ratio, false weighted-grade derivation, unstated minimum in the bus stem, false parallel-timing equality, unsupported causal conclusion, real-square multiple answers, equivalent wrong 2/two choices, and the value question with three equivalent half-valued answers.

The semicolon failure is substantive, not a schema or indexing failure. The original asks only which sentence correctly joins independent clauses with a semicolon. The auditor declared the semicolon-before-however form uniquely correct and called the comma-however-semicolon alternative a misplaced-semicolon error. A conjunctive adverb can attach to the first clause, separated by a comma, with a semicolon between the clauses. This alternate attachment is explicitly illustrated in Bruce Aune's rule R2 (printed page5). The original gold therefore requires rejection; no adjudication of the semicolon-and option is necessary to demonstrate the defect. The sound paired stem explicitly requests however at the beginning of the second clause and was correctly accepted. [Primary grammar source](https://cse.buffalo.edu/~rapaport/Papers/Papers.by.Others/aune01-punctuation.pdf).

Because the auditor sees only already frozen content and emits no replacement teaching, this failure would allow that unchanged false feedback through a future final gate.

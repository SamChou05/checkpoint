# Selector-balanced bank: chronological 20-item blind result

The [pinned offline replay](PREPARATION.md) uses source `01439703d647ab17246c4f9c821a4dd05b5b2a99` and the same accepted author-task seed as the [preceding source replay](../combined-current-bank-replay-20260927/RESULTS.md). The updated selectors constructed 20/40/80 unique exact stems with 20/40/80 code-owned single keys and four literal-distinct choices. Same numeric solve-signature pairs fell from **1/14/78 to 0/12/72**, including cross-family signature pairs from **1/4/18 to 0/2/8**. Same prompt-format pairs within slots fell from **30/133/561 to 26/125/552**. Same-family pairs rose from 0/17/102 to 1/18/106, a deliberate tradeoff from prioritizing solve signatures and spacing English formats. These are structural counts, not semantic-duplicate rates.

The [keyless worksheet](worksheet.json) freezes the **first chronological 20 items** (SHA-256 `987217dc30c7694f3afb57c1c932a0595244002f281fa4ccb0437c04ff430587`) before answer disclosure. Two independent answer-blind reviews ([A](blind-review-a.json), [B](blind-review-b.json)) each cover 20 item solutions, 120 within-item choice pairs, and all 190 cross-item pairs. Both selected all 20 [code-owned keys](post-lock-answer-map.json), found no second viable key, rated every item at difficulty 2–3, and judged every within-item choice pair meaningfully distinct. The [post-lock audit](post-lock-audit.json) checked all 20 main explanations and 80 per-choice explanations and found no factual or key error. It noted thin count-distractor feedback on Q06/Q13/Q20 and a generic out-of-domain phrase on Q17 even though its listed distractors are in the domain; these are teaching-quality limitations, not alternate keys.

Both reviewers rated **four cross-item pairs strongly repetitive**: Q04/Q10 and Q05/Q12 are closely related fraction-expression tasks; Q08/Q17 reduce to choosing an integer solution of a linear equality; Q07/Q09 repeat the same four agreement traps in full-sentence selection format. Reviewer A rated four pairs strong overall, while reviewer B rated 52 strong, mainly because B treated repeated response formats as stronger similarity. The four-pair consensus is therefore a conservative shared signal, not proof that the other 186 pairs are acceptably varied. The preceding blind worksheet was stratified by family over the first 40 items, whereas this one is chronological; their blind strong-pair counts are **not** a matched before/after causal comparison.

The selector does improve a measurable first-batch weakness: slot 4 now supplies a full-sentence question beside slot 3's two-blank question, and the first 20 English items have 16 same-format pairs rather than 28 on the previous source. Yet the two full-sentence questions themselves form a blind-confirmed strong repeat, and 40/80-item banks necessarily reuse this finite repertoire. A broader set of genuinely different objectives and question forms is needed before claiming bank-wide novelty. This is offline constructor evidence, not a full-worker or deployed-product qualification.
